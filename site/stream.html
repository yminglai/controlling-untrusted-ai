    How this is collected

    The stream was seeded by a verified sweep and is refreshed by a scheduled collector that reads RSS feeds and search results every six hours. A language model classifies each candidate into the survey taxonomy. Every item is fetched and checked before publication; items that cannot be verified are not published. Reader submissions arrive through a GitHub issue and go through the same check.
